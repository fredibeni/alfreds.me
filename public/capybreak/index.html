Mac menu bar app

CapyBreak

A furry reminder to get up regularly.

A private Mac menu bar timer that nudges you to move, with flexible intervals, a playful pixel-art fitness capybara and optional reminder exceptions.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store Apple silicon - macOS 13 or later

How it works

CapyBreak lives in your Mac's menu bar and starts a sitting timer while you work. Keyboard presses, clicks and scrolls count as activity, while mouse movement on its own does not. When time is up, follow the fitness capybara or choose a simple Classic reminder, then move through a countdown you configure.

See CapyBreak in action

Built for a calmer workday

Menu bar first

See the current timer when clicking the menu bar icon. CapyBreak stays out of the Dock.

Two reminder styles

Pick the motivational fitness capybara or a simple Classic timer.

Real input signals

Keyboard presses, clicks and scrolls count as activity. Pointer-only mouse movement is ignored.

Movement on your schedule

Configure sitting, away and movement intervals, launch at login and the optional completion sound.

Reminder exceptions

Delay a due reminder when you are in a meeting, presenting slides, playing a game or watching shows.

No account or cloud service

The app has no account, advertising, analytics, telemetry, cloud storage or networking feature.

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 August 2026

CapyBreak works locally on your Mac. It has no account, advertising, analytics, telemetry, cloud storage or networking feature. The app does not transmit personal data, activity information, browser addresses or settings to the developer or any third party.

On-device information

CapyBreak stores your timer settings, enabled features and reminder-exception lists in its macOS sandbox. They remain on your Mac.

While the timer is on, CapyBreak uses aggregate system event counters to detect keyboard presses, mouse clicks and scrolling. It does not read or store the keys you press, typed text, pointer position, documents, screenshots, clipboard contents or activity history. Mouse movement alone is ignored.

When a reminder is due or delayed, CapyBreak may check application and full-screen window state, camera or microphone use, and, if you enable website exceptions, addresses of open Safari and Chrome tabs. These checks decide whether a reminder exception applies. CapyBreak does not capture application content, audio or video. Browser addresses are compared locally with your enabled list and immediately discarded.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transmit personal information and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or data service.

Retention and controls

Activity signals, device-use state, application and window state, and browser addresses are used temporarily and not retained. Local settings remain until you change them or remove CapyBreak's app data.

You can turn the timer off or quit CapyBreak to stop checks, disable reminder exceptions, revoke browser access in macOS System Settings, and disable Launch at login. To remove all local settings, turn off Launch at login, quit CapyBreak, then remove ~/Library/Containers/com.capybreak.app.

Accessibility

The exact Mac App Store accessibility labels will be selected only after their required manual tests pass on the submitted build.

  • Native, labelled Yes and No controls with a spoken selected state.
  • Keyboard navigation that keeps focus and the visible answer selection aligned.
  • A high-priority VoiceOver announcement when the movement countdown succeeds.
  • Reduce Motion support that replaces the repeating exercise animation with a still pose.
  • VoiceOver actions that move the draggable capybara reminder or return it to the centre.
  • Contextual spoken labels for timer information and bulk application or website actions.
  • State text and menu bar silhouettes that do not depend on colour alone.
  • A semantic accent colour chosen for readable contrast on light and dark system backgrounds.

For accessibility feedback, email hello@alfreds.me.
