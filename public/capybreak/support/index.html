Compatibility

CapyBreak 1.0.0 requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later.

Where is CapyBreak after I open it?

CapyBreak is a menu bar app and does not show a Dock icon. Look for the capybara icon at the top of the screen. Click it to see the timer, then click the cog to open Settings.

Why did the sitting timer restart?

CapyBreak starts a fresh sitting interval after there has been no keyboard press, mouse click or scroll for longer than the away threshold and activity is next detected. Pointer movement alone does not count, which prevents pointer-only mouse movement from keeping the timer active.

Why was a reminder delayed?

Enabled reminder exceptions can delay a due reminder for selected applications, matching website domains, a full-screen presentation or game, or camera or microphone use. Review each toggle and list in Settings. CapyBreak rechecks a delayed reminder approximately every 5 seconds and presents it when the exception ends, provided the sitting cycle is still active.

Why did macOS ask to control Safari or Chrome?

Website reminder exceptions use macOS Automation permission to ask each running supported browser for open tab addresses only while a reminder is due or delayed. Addresses are matched locally and immediately discarded. CapyBreak never changes a tab or browser window.

You may deny access and keep using the timer without website detection. To change the decision later, open System Settings, select Privacy & Security, then Automation.

How do I change launch at login?

Turn Launch at login on or off in CapyBreak Settings. You can also manage login items in macOS System Settings under General, then Login Items & Extensions.

How do I stop CapyBreak checking activity?

Turn the timer off from the menu bar popup, or quit CapyBreak. Turning the timer off stops reminder monitoring while leaving the app available in the menu bar.

How do I remove my settings?

Turn off Launch at login in CapyBreak Settings, quit CapyBreak, then in Finder choose Go > Go to Folder and remove ~/Library/Containers/com.capybreak.app.

CapyBreak has no account and holds no server-side copy of your settings.

What should I include in a support request?

Include your macOS version, Mac model, CapyBreak version, what you expected, what happened and the shortest steps that reproduce the issue. Please remove personal information from screenshots and do not send browser addresses, passwords or other sensitive information unless necessary.

Privacy

The CapyBreak app works locally and does not transmit personal data, browser addresses, activity information or settings. Read the full CapyBreak Privacy Policy.
